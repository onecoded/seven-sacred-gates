Privacy — local first, by design

The app runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, journal entries, dreams, practice logs, and reading live in your device's local storage — we run no accounts, no tracking pixels, and no analytics on your healing data. If you enable the optional cloud vault, your data is encrypted on your device before upload with a key that never leaves your possession; the server stores ciphertext under an anonymous identifier and cannot read it. Losing your soul key means losing access to the synced copy — keep a file backup too. If you connect the Oracle to an AI backend, the messages you type and a summary of your recorded data are sent to that backend to generate replies.
